**TL;DR:** Stuck? Check [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting), then ask in the community Discord or file a bug. Want to help? Sponsor, star the repo, or send a fix. WolfWave is free, open-source, and built by one person on the side.

## Get help

1. **Check the docs first.** [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) and the [FAQ](/docs/faq) cover the common problems.
2. **Ask in chat.** Join the [community Discord](https://mrdwolf.net/discord).
3. **Still broken?** [Report a bug](#report-a-bug).

## Report a bug

A clear report saves hours. The fastest way is from inside WolfWave:

1. Open the bug form, either:
   - **menu bar icon → Help → Report a Bug**, or
   - **Settings → About → Send Feedback**.
2. Your browser opens a GitHub issue. Your WolfWave version and Mac details are already filled in.
3. Add what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Then submit.

Want to attach logs?

1. Open **Settings → Advanced**.
2. Under **Diagnostics**, click **Export Logs**. This saves one file with your Mac details, any crash note, and the app logs.
3. Look it over, then attach it to the issue.

Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to share it. See the [Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy-policy).

No WolfWave handy? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/MrDemonWolf/wolfwave/issues/new) directly.
