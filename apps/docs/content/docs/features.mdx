**TL;DR:** A tour of every WolfWave feature for streamers. Each section says what it does, how to turn it on, and where to read more.

Haven't installed yet? Start with [Install](/docs/installation).

## Menu bar and Music Sync

WolfWave lives in your menu bar. Click the wolf icon to open the menu. It rebuilds each time you open it, so it always matches what's happening now.

**What's in the menu**, top to bottom:

- **Now playing.** The song, artist, and album art. Hidden when Music has no song loaded.
- **Music controls.** **Play** or **Pause**, **Next Track**, and **Previous Track**, right under the song. They show only while Music is open, so the menu never launches it.
- **Share Song** is a submenu:
  - **Copy Song Info** copies the title and artist.
  - **Copy Song Link** copies a link that opens on any music service. It's greyed out and says "Resolving…" while it looks one up, or "No link found" if there is none.
  - **Send to Twitch Chat** posts what `!song` would say. Only while Twitch is connected.
- **Recently Played** is a submenu. Click a song to copy it.
- **Song requests.** Only when song requests are on. **Hold Song Requests** (or **Resume Song Requests**) is always on top, then **Show Request Queue…**, which opens the [Request Queue window](#request-queue-window) and says how many requests need your approval. When there's something to act on, you also get the request playing now, the queue count, **Skip Song Request**, and **Clear Queue…**. A busy queue folds those into a **Song Requests (N)** submenu.
- **Quick switches.** Apple Music, Twitch, Discord, and OBS Overlay, each with a short status. A check mark means on. Twitch shows once you've signed in. Click it to leave your channel. If you're not connected, it opens **Settings → Twitch** instead.
- **Streamer tools.** **Streamer Mode**, **Mute Bot** (stops chat messages while paid requests continue), and **Copy Widget Link** (only while the overlay is on).
- **App.** **Settings…**, **Help**, and **Quit WolfWave**. **Help** holds **About WolfWave**, **What's New**, **Check for Updates…**, **Documentation**, **Community Discord**, **Report a Bug**, **Star on GitHub**, and **Sponsor WolfWave**. Turn off **Show sponsor links** in [Settings → About](/docs/settings#about) to hide Sponsor. At the bottom of **Help**: **Open Widget in Browser** (only while the overlay is on), and **Restart Integrations…**, which reconnects Twitch, Discord, and the overlay (whichever are on) after you confirm. Viewers may see a short gap.

**Clear Queue…** empties the queue and **WolfWave Requests** together after you confirm. If any requests are queued, waiting, or playing, it stops Music too. If there are none, Music keeps playing. The songs stay in your Apple Music library; the `!playlist` link shows an empty playlist until new requests arrive. If Music can't clear the playlist, an alert says **WolfWave Requests couldn't be cleared**. The queue is already clear by then.

**Dock menu.** Right-click the WolfWave icon in the Dock for a shorter menu: the song playing, **Play** or **Pause**, **Next Track**, and **Previous Track** (only while Music is open), **Hold Song Requests** or **Resume Song Requests** and **Show Request Queue…** (only when requests are on), check-mark switches for Apple Music, Twitch (after you sign in), Discord, and OBS Overlay, and **Settings…**. It's there whenever **Display mode** shows the Dock (**Dock and Menu Bar** or **Dock Only**). Dock Only hides the menu bar icon, so this menu is your shortcut. Set Display mode in [Settings → General](/docs/settings#general).

How it works:

- **Music Sync** is the main switch. It's in **Settings → General → Sync Music**. It's on by default.
- **Apple Music only.** WolfWave asks Apple Music directly. It doesn't read other players.
- **Battery friendly.** It checks less often in Low Power Mode.
- **Asks before risky actions.** **Clear Queue…** and **Restart Integrations…** confirm first.

## Twitch chat bot

Let chat ask what's playing.

**Turn it on:**

1. Open **Settings → Twitch** and click **Connect with Twitch**.
2. Click **Open twitch.tv/activate**, enter the code, and approve. If the code expires, click **Get a new code**. You never type your password into WolfWave.
3. Type your channel name and click **Join Channel**.
4. Under **Bot Commands**, switch on the commands you want.

**What you get:**

- **`!song`** posts the current song. **`!last`** posts the one before. Both are on for new installs, so they answer as soon as you join your channel. If you were already set up when you updated, they stay as you had them. Switch either off any time under **Bot Commands**.
- **`!wolfwave`** tells chat what WolfWave is. Off by default.
- **Add a song.link** puts a link on `!song` and `!last` replies that opens the song on any music service. Off by default, because some chats delete messages with links. It shows up once `!song` or `!last` is on.
- **Aliases.** Add your own extra names for a command.
- **Cooldowns** stop spam. They don't apply to you or your mods.
- **Only reply while live.** Turn this on and every command stays quiet until you go live. Off by default.
- **Custom commands.** Make your own `!command` with a fixed reply. Variables like `$user`, `$touser`, `$args`, `$song`, and `$lastsong` fill in live. Each one has a permission level, a cooldown, and a choice of reply, plain message, or Twitch announcement. You can set them up before you connect.

Full list and defaults: [Bot Commands](/docs/bot-commands).

## Song requests

Let chat add songs to a queue that plays in Apple Music.

**Turn it on:** connect Twitch first (see above), then open **Settings → Song Requests** and click **Set Up Song Requests**. Each setup step is in [Song Requests settings](/docs/settings#song-requests).

Requests can't turn on until setup is done. Nothing half-set-up goes live.

**How viewers request:**

- **`!sr <song name>`** searches Apple Music.
- **`!sr <link>`** works with Apple Music, Spotify, and YouTube links. WolfWave finds the matching Apple Music song.
- If a Spotify or YouTube link is unclear, WolfWave says no instead of guessing. Ask for the artist and title instead.

**Who can request** (the **Who Can Request** card):

| Preset | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Anyone can request: `!sr`, channel points, or Bits. |
| **Sub Only** | Only subscribers. Channel points and Bits are off. |
| **Channel Point Only** | `!sr` is off. Viewers redeem the channel-point reward. |
| **Custom** | You choose who can use `!sr`: Everyone, Subscribers, VIPs & Subscribers, or Mods Only. |

Mods and you can always request.

**Require My Approval** (same card) holds every request until you approve or decline it in the queue. Off by default.

**Queue rules** (the **Queue Settings** card):

- **Max queue size.** 10 songs by default.
- **Per-user limits.** How many songs one viewer can have waiting. 2 by default. You can set a different limit per role.
- **Fair-Share Ordering.** Everyone's first request plays before anyone's second. On by default. Turn it off for first-come, first-served.
- **Sub / VIP priority.** Off by default. **Skip cooldown** lets subs, VIPs, and mods skip the wait. **Jump the queue** also moves them ahead in each round.

**Playback** (the **Playback** card):

- **Auto-Advance Queue** plays the next request when a song ends.
- **Hold Queue** pauses the queue. Requests still come in, but nothing plays until you resume. Also in the menu bar and the `!hold` / `!resume` commands.
- **Fallback playlist** plays an Apple Music playlist when the queue is empty. Type its name exactly as it appears in Music. Leave it blank for silence.
- **Resume Apple Music AutoPlay** hands playback back to Music when the queue empties. Turn on AutoPlay in Music's Playing Next panel first. A fallback playlist takes priority.

### Request Queue window [#request-queue-window]

Manage requests mid-stream without opening Settings. Open it from **Show Request Queue…** in the menu bar or Dock menu, or **Open in Window** on the Settings queue.

- **Sections.** **Playing Now**, **Needs Approval**, and **Up Next** with a rough total wait. Rows show the album art.
- **Right-click** a request for what applies: **Approve**, **Decline**, **Play Next**, **Remove**, **Skip**, **Open in Apple Music**, **Block Song**, or **Block** the requester. Select several to approve or remove them together.
- **Drag** queued songs to reorder them. **Play Next** does the same without dragging.
- **Keys.** **Delete** removes the selected requests (or declines waiting ones). Double-click approves a waiting request or plays a queued one next. **⌘→** skips.
- **Toolbar.** **Skip**, **Hold** / **Resume**, **Keep on Top** (floats the window over your game or OBS), and **Clear Requests**.
- Removing or declining a channel-point request refunds the points.

**Content filters** (the **Content Filters** card):

- **Block Explicit Songs** turns away songs Apple Music marks explicit and tells the viewer to try the clean version.
- **Max song length** turns away longer songs and says how long the song is and what the max is.
- New setups start with explicit songs blocked and a 6-minute max. If you set up before these existed, both stay off until you turn them on.
- You need the rights to play music on stream. Read the [Twitch Music Guidelines](https://legal.twitch.com/legal/music/).

**Blocklist:**

- Block a **Song**, an **Artist**, or a **Person**.
- A blocked person is turned away before WolfWave even searches.
- Matching ignores capital letters, accents, and extra spaces.
- Artist blocks also catch collaborations (for example, a blocked artist inside "Artist A feat. Artist B").
- Heads up: it matches text. A short entry like `Wind` also blocks "Earth, Wind & Fire".

**Vote-to-skip:**

- Turn on **Enable Vote-Skip**. Chat types `!voteskip` (or `!vs`).
- Set the **Minimum votes to skip** (3 by default) and, for chat votes, the vote window (60 seconds by default).
- Optional: **Subscriber-Only Voting**, or **Use Twitch Polls** for a native poll (Affiliate/Partner only).

**Sync Library check:**

- Songs play from the Music app on this Mac.
- The **WolfWave Requests** playlist only reaches Music if **Sync Library** is on (**Music → Settings → General**).
- WolfWave gives iCloud up to about 40 seconds to sync, then warns you if Music still can't see the playlist.

More: chat commands in [Bot Commands](/docs/bot-commands), every switch in [Settings](/docs/settings#song-requests).

### Channel points and Bits

These live in the **Channel Points & Bits** card. Enable the tools on Twitch, sign in with your broadcaster account, and grant the permissions WolfWave requests. Twitch must allow the API access needed for each feature; see the [FAQ](/docs/faq#requests-and-chat) for eligibility details.

- **Channel Point Requests.** WolfWave makes a "Request a Song" reward for you (500 points by default). Points are only spent when the song plays. If a request fails (not found, blocked, explicit, too long, queue full) or the song is removed before it plays (`!wrongsong`, declined, removed, queue cleared, can't play), the points go back automatically. **Repair Reward** checks the reward with Twitch and makes a fresh one if you deleted it.
- **Bit Requests.** Viewers cheer with a song name to request it. Set the **Minimum bits** (100 by default).
- **Boost With Bits.** A cheer moves the viewer's oldest queued song to the front instead of adding a new one.
- **Mostly the same rules as `!sr`.** Points and Bits follow your blocklist, content filters, approval, and hold settings. **Who Can Request** applies to `!sr` only, and paid requests use the **Everyone** per-user limit.
- **Safe across crashes.** WolfWave saves what it owes before it touches the queue. After a quit or outage it finishes the refund or the Bits action. The queue doesn't survive a quit, so channel-point songs that hadn't played yet are refunded on the next launch. If you sign out of Twitch while paid songs are waiting, they stay queued and the points are spent.

## Now-playing widget (OBS overlay) [#now-playing-widget]

Show the current song on stream. It runs on your Mac. No overlay website needed.

- **One link.** Turn on **Enable Stream Widgets** and **Enable Widget Webpage** in **Settings → Stream Widgets**, click **Copy Link**, and paste it into an OBS **Browser Source**.
- **Your look.** Five themes (Default, Dark, Light, Glass, Neon) and five layouts (Horizontal, Vertical, Compact, Vinyl, Classic), with a live preview.
- **Queue ticker.** Show the next requests on screen.

Setup, sizes, and extras: [OBS Overlay](/docs/obs-overlay).

## Stream Deck

Control WolfWave from a Stream Deck. The official plugin has 13 keys: play/pause, skip, hold the queue, approve, reject, clear the queue, block a song or requester, change who can request, announce the song, toggle the overlay, show what's playing, and display track time.

- Every key shows live state.
- It uses its own control token that only works on this Mac.

Setup: [Stream Deck](/docs/streamdeck). Building your own tool? See the [Stream Deck Control API](/docs/stream-deck).

## Discord status

Show "Listening to WolfWave" on your Discord profile, with album art and a live progress bar.

**Turn it on:**

1. Open the Discord app on this Mac.
2. Open **Settings → Discord** and turn on **Show on Discord**.

No bot, no token, no server. Off by default.

**Options:**

- **Profile Buttons.** Up to two buttons under your song: an **Apple Music link** and an **Any-app link (song.link)**. On by default. **Show buttons** turns them all off at once.
- **Show playlist.** Off by default. Shows the playlist you're playing from, either next to the artist or as a tooltip on the app icon (**Display style**). **Show playlist name** off keeps the name private.
- **When you pause.** **Keep the song** (default) leaves it up with a pause badge. While it plays, the badge is a play icon. **Hide the song** shows your "When music stops" choice instead.
- **When music stops.** **Listening to WolfWave** (default) shows that line with no song. **Nothing** clears your status.

## Listening history and stats

- **Off by default.** Turn on **Listening History** in **Settings → History & Stats** to start recording plays.
- **Stays on your Mac.** It's never uploaded.
- **Counts real plays.** A song counts after half its length or 4 minutes, so skips don't count.
- **Charts are a second switch.** Turn on **Show Stats & Charts** for top artists, listening by hour, and the Monthly Wrap, a monthly summary you can share as an image. It needs Listening History on first.
- **`!stats` for chat** has its own switch, off by default. Chat can use it once Twitch is connected. Pick the time window and what it shows.
- **Keep for** clears old plays on a schedule, from 7 days up to a year. **Forever** keeps them all.

More: [Listening History](/docs/listening-history).

## Notifications

Mac banners for:

- **Song changes** (with album art).
- **Skip vote started** and **Skip vote passed** (need vote-to-skip on).

All off by default. Turn them on in [Settings → General](/docs/settings#general). A new banner replaces the old one of the same kind.

One more banner has no switch: **Twitch Authentication Expired** shows up if your Twitch sign-in lapses, as long as macOS already lets WolfWave post notifications.

## App basics

Menu bar or Dock, **Launch at Login**, light or dark, and updates (**Stable** or **Nightly**) are all in [Settings](/docs/settings#general).

## Streamer Mode

One click in the menu bar hides private info on screen. Your channel name shows as **hidden**. Overlay links and tokens show as **hidden (streamer mode)**. Everything keeps working. More: [Streamer Mode](/docs/streamer-mode).

## Back up, restore, and iCloud sync

- **Export Settings** saves your preferences to a file. **Import Settings** brings them back on a new Mac.
- **Sync with iCloud** keeps your preferences matched across your Macs.
- Sign-ins and tokens are never included. You sign in to Twitch once per Mac.

More: [Back Up & Restore](/docs/backup).

## Help, privacy, and security

- **Something broke?** **Report a Bug** (**menu bar → Help**) opens a GitHub issue with your version filled in, and **Export Logs** saves one file to attach. More: [Report a bug](/docs/support#report-a-bug) and [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
- **Your data stays yours.** Sign-ins live in the macOS Keychain. No analytics. Data only goes to services you turn on. More: [Security](/docs/security) and the [Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy-policy).
- **Comparing tools?** See the [comparison](/docs/comparison).
