**TL;DR:** For streamers setting up WolfWave for the first time. Install the app, follow the setup wizard, then turn on the features you want.

## What you need

- **macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later.** Older versions can't run it.
- **A Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1 or later). Intel Macs aren't supported.
- **The Apple Music app.** It's built into macOS. WolfWave only reads from Apple Music, not Spotify or other players.
- **For song requests:** an Apple Music subscription, with **Sync Library** on in Music → Settings → General.

2. **Drag WolfWave into Applications.**
3. **Open WolfWave** from Applications or Spotlight.

The app updates itself. You can check any time from **Settings → Software Update**.

</Tab>

<Tab value="Homebrew">

**For people who already use [Homebrew](https://brew.sh).**

```bash
brew install --cask mrdemonwolf/den/wolfwave
```

Homebrew handles updates. The in-app updater turns itself off for this install. To update, run:

```bash
brew upgrade wolfwave
```

## Step 2: The setup wizard

The wizard opens the first time you launch WolfWave. It has 7 short steps and takes about two minutes.

- The top of the window says where you are, like **Step 3 of 7 · Discord**.
- **Next** moves on. On Apple Music and Twitch it reads **Skip** until you finish that step. Press **Return** for it (on Twitch, Return joins your channel instead).
- **Back** (or **Escape**) returns to the step before.
- **Skip setup** ends the wizard now. What you already switched on stays on, and the wizard won't come back. Close the window instead and it opens again next launch.
- You can finish anything later in **Settings**, or open the wizard again from **Settings → Advanced → Rerun Setup Wizard**. Rerunning keeps your settings. Each step shows what you have now.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Welcome

A quick hello. WolfWave shares only what you turn on.

</Step>

<Step>

### Apple Music

**This one matters.** It lets WolfWave see the song that's playing.

1. Click **Allow Apple Music access**.
2. Click **OK** when macOS asks.

Clicked "Don't Allow" by mistake? Click **Open System Settings**, go to **Privacy & Security → Automation → WolfWave**, turn on **Music**, then come back. WolfWave checks again on its own, or click **Check again**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Discord

Turn on **Show on Discord** to show your song on your Discord profile.

- The step tells you whether Discord is open. WolfWave talks to it directly, so it has to be.
- **Listen buttons** ("Listen on Apple Music" and a link for any other music app) appear once **Show on Discord** is on. They start on. One tap turns them off.
- No bot or token needed.

</Step>

<Step>

### Twitch

1. Click **Connect with Twitch**.
2. WolfWave shows a code. Click **Open twitch.tv/activate**, enter the code, and approve.
3. **Which channel should WolfWave join?** starts filled in with your Twitch name. Change it if chat lives on another channel, then click **Join**. When it works, the row reads **In your chat**.

Under **Chat commands**, `!song` and `!last` each have a switch. On a new install both are already on, so chat can ask what's playing right away. Turn either off here.

Used WolfWave before? They stay as you had them. More commands and cooldowns are in **Settings → Twitch**.

Your sign-in is saved in your Mac's Keychain. You never type your Twitch password into WolfWave.

</Step>

<Step>

### Stream overlay

1. Turn on **Stream overlay**. This one switch covers **Enable Stream Widgets** and **Enable Widget Webpage** in **Settings → Stream Widgets**.
2. Click the copy button next to **Overlay link**.
3. In OBS, add a **Browser Source** and paste the link.

**Link hidden?** [Streamer Mode](/docs/streamer-mode) hides it and turns the copy button off. Turn it off in the menu bar, copy the link, then turn it back on.

Sizes and styles are in [Stream Widgets](/docs/features#now-playing-widget).

</Step>

<Step>

### Alerts

1. Click **Allow notifications**. macOS asks once.
2. Pick your alerts: **Song changes**, **Skip vote started**, **Skip vote passed**.

All are off unless you turn them on, and the switches stay dimmed until notifications are allowed.

- **Said no before?** The step says notifications are off for WolfWave. Click **Open System Settings**, turn them on, then come back.
- **Already allowed?** The step says so. Just pick your alerts.
- The two skip-vote alerts only fire once **Enable Vote-Skip** is on in **Settings → Song Requests**.

Change these later in **Settings → General → Notifications**. If macOS has them blocked, that pane shows an **Open System Settings** button too.

</Step>

<Step>

### Mac settings

Three switches, all off until you turn them on:

- **Start WolfWave at login.**
- **Remember my listening history.** Kept on this Mac only. Powers [History & Stats](/docs/listening-history).
- **Sync settings with iCloud.** Your preferences follow you to your other Macs. Accounts stay on this one. See [Back Up & Restore](/docs/backup).

</Step>

</Steps>

Click **Finish**. The last screen points at the wolf icon in your menu bar: click it any time to see what's playing or open **Settings**. Click **Done** (or wait a few seconds) and you're set.
