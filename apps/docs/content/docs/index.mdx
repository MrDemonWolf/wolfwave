## Quick setup

<Tabs items={["Connect Twitch", "OBS overlay", "Song requests", "Discord status"]}>

<Tab value="Connect Twitch">

<Steps>

<Step>

**Open Settings → Twitch.** Click **Connect with Twitch**.

</Step>

<Step>

**Approve on twitch.tv/activate.** Enter the code WolfWave shows you. You never type your Twitch password into WolfWave.

</Step>

<Step>

**Join your channel, then pick commands.** Type your channel name and click **Join Channel**. Then switch on the commands you want under **Bot Commands**. `!song` and `!last` start off.

</Step>

</Steps>

More: [Twitch chat bot](/docs/features#twitch-chat-bot) · [Every command](/docs/bot-commands)

</Tab>

<Tab value="OBS overlay">

<Steps>

<Step>

**Open Settings → Stream Widgets.** Turn on **Enable Stream Widgets**, then **Enable Widget Webpage**.

</Step>

<Step>

**Click Copy Link.** This is the localhost link for OBS.

</Step>

<Step>

**Add it to OBS as a Browser Source.** Use the width and height the pane shows for your layout.

</Step>

</Steps>

More: [OBS overlay](/docs/features#now-playing-widget) · [Themes & layouts](/docs/widget)

</Tab>

<Tab value="Song requests">

<Steps>

<Step>

**Open Settings → Song Requests.** Click **Set Up Song Requests**. It checks Twitch, asks for Apple Music access, and makes your **WolfWave Requests** playlist.

</Step>

<Step>

**Pick who can request.** Choose a preset: Open, Sub Only, Channel Point Only, or Custom.

</Step>

<Step>

**Chat types `!sr <song>`.** WolfWave finds it on Apple Music and adds it to the queue.

</Step>

</Steps>

More: [Song requests](/docs/features#song-requests) · [Vote-to-skip](/docs/bot-commands#vote-skip)

</Tab>

<Tab value="Discord status">

<Steps>

<Step>

**Open the Discord app** on this Mac.

</Step>

<Step>

**Open Settings → Discord.** Turn on **Show on Discord**.

</Step>

<Step>

**Check your profile.** It shows "Listening to WolfWave" with the album art and a progress bar. No bot or token needed.

</Step>

</Steps>

More: [Discord status](/docs/features#discord-status)

</Tab>

</Tabs>
