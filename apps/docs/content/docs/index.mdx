## Get started

[Install WolfWave](/docs/installation), then follow the setup wizard. It walks you through Apple Music, Twitch, Discord, and the overlay.

## Get help

Start with [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) or the [FAQ](/docs/faq). Still stuck? Ask in the [community Discord](https://mrdwolf.net/discord) or [report a bug](/docs/support#report-a-bug).

## For developers

[Build WolfWave from source](/docs/development), or connect your own tools to the [overlay API](/docs/widget#message-contract) and the [Stream Deck API](/docs/stream-deck).
