**TL;DR:** Streamer Mode hides private details inside WolfWave so you can show the app on stream. It only changes what's on your screen. This page is for streamers.

## Turn it on

1. Click the WolfWave icon in the menu bar.
2. Click **Streamer Mode**. A checkmark means it's on.

Click it again to turn it off. WolfWave remembers your choice, even after a restart.

- **No menu bar icon?** **Dock Only** hides it, and the Dock menu has no Streamer Mode item. Pick **Dock and Menu Bar** or **Menu Bar Only** in **Settings → General → App Visibility** first.
- **Using iCloud?** With **Sync with iCloud**, this switch follows you to your other synced Macs. Turn it off on one and it turns off on the rest, so check it before you go live.

## What it hides

With Streamer Mode on, these show as **hidden** or **hidden (streamer mode)** instead of the real value:

- **Your Twitch channel name and signed-in username**, in Settings, the menu bar, and Twitch error messages.
- **Overlay and widget links**, in Stream Widgets, the setup wizard, and **Integrations** on **Settings → General**.
- **WebSocket addresses.**
- **Your Mac's local network address**, in the **Network Address** links and the Stream Widgets status line.
- **The channel-point reward ID** (**Managed reward ID**) in Song Requests.

A **Streamer Mode** badge sits next to hidden links, tokens, and your channel, so nobody mistakes them for a bug.

### Tokens are locked too

The **Overlay Token** (**Settings → Stream Widgets**) and the **Stream Deck Control Token** (**Settings → Stream Deck**) stay hidden. While Streamer Mode is on you can't:

- reveal them,
- copy them,
- edit them, or
- regenerate them.

### Copy and Open are off

In Settings, the **Copy** and **Open** buttons next to a hidden link stop working. A stray click can't paste it into chat or open it in a browser.

The menu bar's **Copy Widget Link** and **Open Widget in Browser** still work, so a stray click there can still paste the link or open it. They only use the plain `localhost` link, never your token or network address.

## What it does NOT change

Streamer Mode only changes the screen. Everything else keeps running:

- **Your overlay** keeps getting now-playing data.
- **Discord Rich Presence** keeps updating.
- **Twitch chat replies** are the same.

Leave it on for your whole stream. There's no downside.

## When to use it

- Sharing your whole desktop.
- Showing Settings on camera.
- Recording a tutorial or a bug-report screenshot.

Need to copy a token or link, or type your Twitch channel (its field is hidden while you're signed out)? Turn Streamer Mode off for a moment, do it off-camera, then turn it back on.
