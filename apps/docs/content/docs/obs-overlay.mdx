**TL;DR:** Turn on Stream Widgets, copy one link, and paste it into OBS as a Browser Source. The overlay runs on your Mac. This page is for streamers.

## Add it to OBS

<Steps>

<Step>Open **Settings → Stream Widgets** and turn on **Enable Stream Widgets**.</Step>
<Step>In **Widget Setup**, turn on **Enable Widget Webpage**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Copy Link**.</Step>
<Step>In OBS, add a **Browser Source** and paste the link.</Step>
<Step>Set the width and height from the [size table](#pick-a-size) for your layout.</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  In OBS, turn on **Shutdown source when not visible**. The widget then reconnects cleanly when you switch scenes.
</Callout>

Play a song in Apple Music. The card should appear within a few seconds.

## Pick a size

The widget keeps 16 px of clear space on every side, so the Browser Source is a little bigger than the card.

| Layout | Looks like | OBS width × height |
|---|---|---:|
| Horizontal | Wide bar with art on the left | 532 × 132 |
| Vertical | Tall card with art on top | 252 × 312 |
| Compact | Thin one-line strip | 382 × 88 |
| Vinyl | Spinning record with a progress ring | 292 × 332 |
| Classic | Album tile beside a title card | 472 × 144 |

## Change the look

Everything is in the **Widget Appearance** card of **Settings → Stream Widgets**.

- **Theme:** Default, Dark, Light, Glass, or Neon.
- **Layout:** Horizontal, Vertical, Compact, Vinyl, or Classic.
- **Text** and **Background** colors (Default and Glass themes only), and a **Font**.

A live preview shows your edits as you make them. Nothing reaches OBS until you click **Apply**. **Revert** throws your edits away. The overlay restyles on its own, so you never edit the link or refresh OBS.

Changed layouts? Update the Browser Source size to match.

## What viewers see

| When | The overlay |
|---|---|
| A song is playing | Shows the song, artist, art, and progress |
| You pause | Stays up, dims the art, and freezes the progress bar |
| Music stops or Apple Music quits | Clears the card |
| You press the Stream Deck **Show / Hide Overlay** key | Hides or shows the card |

## Extras

Add these to the end of your link. Start the first one with `?` and any after it with `&`.

| Add this | What it does |
|---|---|
| `queueTicker=1` | Shows the next 3 song requests in the bottom-right corner on this source. Easier: turn on **Show Up Next on Overlay** in **Settings → Song Requests**, which shows it on every overlay without changing the link |
| `duration=8` | Hides the card 8 seconds after each new song starts (use any number) |
| `hideAlbumArt` | Leaves out the album art |

The queue ticker reads "Queue open — !sr to join" when nothing is waiting. It needs [song requests](/docs/features#song-requests) turned on.

## OBS on a second PC

Use the **Network Address** link from **Widget Setup** in place of **Copy Link**. It carries a token, so treat it like a password and only use it on a network you trust.

## It's not working

- **The card never shows.** Check that Apple Music is playing and **Sync Music** is on in **Settings → General**.
- **The Stream Widgets chip says Couldn't start.** Another app is using the port. See [Port already in use](/docs/troubleshooting#port-in-use).
- **It stopped updating.** See [The overlay stopped updating](/docs/troubleshooting#overlay-not-updating).
