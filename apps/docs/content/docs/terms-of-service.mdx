## 2. Description of Service

WolfWave is a macOS menu bar application that:

- Monitors Apple Music playback on your device
- Responds to Twitch chat commands with now-playing information, including custom commands you author
- Accepts song requests from Twitch chat, channel-point redemptions, and bit cheers, with queue, approval, blocklist, and vote-skip controls
- Displays Discord Rich Presence with your current listening activity
- Serves a local now-playing overlay and streams data to WebSocket endpoints for stream overlays and control clients such as a Stream Deck
- Optionally records listening history on your device with stats and monthly summaries
- Supports exporting and importing settings backups, and optionally syncing settings between your Macs through your own iCloud account
- Updates itself via Sparkle on the Stable or opt-in Nightly channel (unless installed via Homebrew)

The App runs entirely on your device and does not require a subscription or account with MrDemonWolf, Inc.
